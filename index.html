Privacy Policy for BillNudge

Last updated: November 7, 2026

BillNudge ("the app") is a bill reminder app developed and operated by Kelip Labs ("we" or "us"), an independent developer and the developer named on the app's Google Play listing. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

In short: your bills stay on your phone. When you ask BillNudge to read a bill or a receipt, that one document is sent through our server to Google's Gemini service to be read, and it isn't kept. Our server keeps only when you scanned, to count your free scans. No ads, no tracking, no bank login.

Information We Collect

Bills and receipts you scan. When you share, choose, snap or paste a bill or a payment receipt and BillNudge reads it, the document (a PDF, a photo, or the text of a message) is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to Google's Gemini API (the paid service, under which Google doesn't use what it's sent to improve its products) to read the biller, amount, due date, account number and payment details. Your phone's time zone setting (for example, "Asia/Kuala_Lumpur") is sent with it, so dates are read the way your country writes them. Bills and receipts contain personal and financial information such as your name, address, account numbers and amounts. Neither our server nor BillNudge keeps the document or what was read from it: it's processed to answer that one request, and the answer comes back to your phone. BillNudge makes photos smaller and cuts a very large PDF to its first three pages on your phone before sending them, and our server sends Gemini only the first three pages of a PDF.

Password-protected PDFs. If a PDF has a password, you type it in the app and the PDF is unlocked on your phone. The password is never sent anywhere and isn't kept.

Things read on your phone only. The camera scanner (Google's ML Kit document scanner, part of Google Play services), the reading of payment QR codes on a bill, and finding a bill's payment web page all happen on your phone; nothing from them is sent to us.

Your bills. The bills you add, their payments, notes and reminders, and the originals and receipts BillNudge keeps for them, are stored on your phone. They are not sent to our server or kept in your account. If your phone's own Android backup to your Google account is on, the bills list (not the kept originals or receipts) is included in that backup, which Google keeps for you under its own policy.

Your account. BillNudge signs you in as a guest automatically, with an anonymous account ID and no personal information, so it can count your free scans. You can choose to sign in with an email address and password, or your Google account, to keep your free scans when you change phones. If you sign in with Google, Firebase receives your name, email address and profile photo as provided by Google; we use them only to identify your account.

Usage data for the free scans. To count free scans, our server stores your account ID, a randomly generated install identifier (not your phone's hardware ID), and the time of each scan. For an hour after a scan it also keeps a random number the app gives each document, so trying again after a lost connection isn't counted twice. Scan times are deleted after 62 days. The date of the first scan for an account and for an install is kept, because the first 3 months have a bigger allowance. Our server also keeps a few totals for each day (how many bills, receipts and other documents were read, how many reads failed and how many were refused for having no free scans left); these totals contain no account IDs, install identifiers or document content.

Server logs. Our server's logs record what kind of document was read (a file or a message), which model read it, how long it took and whether it was a bill, never its content. Our hosting provider may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time.

Files you make. A backup file, an export (PDF or spreadsheet) or a "Send to family" file is made on your phone and goes only where you save or send it. These files contain your account numbers, so keep them to people you trust.

How We Use Information

  • To read the bills and receipts you ask BillNudge to read
  • To count free scans per account and per install
  • To authenticate your account if you sign in
  • To understand, from daily totals, how much the app is used

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except as described here. BillNudge has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools, and it never connects to your bank.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini API — reads the bills and receipts you scan
  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — the guest account and optional sign-in
  • Google ML Kit — the camera scanner and QR code reading, on your phone
  • Google Fonts — the app downloads its typeface from Google's font servers, which receive your device's IP address as part of that request
  • Fly.io — hosts our server, in Singapore

Google's services are subject to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Fly.io's is at https://fly.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your bills stay on your phone until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Deleting a bill deletes the original and receipts kept with it.

Deleting Your BillNudge Account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open the menu on the home screen, then Account, then Delete account. After you confirm your password (or Google account), this permanently deletes your account and our server's records for it (the times of your scans and the date of your first scan). Your bills are on your phone, not in the account, so they stay there. The only thing kept is the anonymous record for that install described above (scan times, deleted after 62 days, and its first-scan date), which isn't linked to your account and keeps the free allowance from being reset. If you no longer have the app installed, you can instead email us at the address below to request deletion, and we'll delete your account and its records manually.

Children's Privacy

BillNudge is intended for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us at the address below and we'll delete it.
